# T141 — large vector sheets lag on the iPad (round 2026-11-01, C-38)

Landry, on the Level 03 composite: "The pdf is very slow to load and lags when moving around. We
need to find a way to handle big files and make the movement seamless. This issue carries over to
the survey tab as well, not just placing devices." D233 (approved 2026-11-02): a heavy display
sheet is rasterised on the server; the threshold and resolution are T141's to propose (D242).

## Measurement (PC, pdfium 156 via pypdfium2)

pdfium is the engine the floor PDF already uses; it stands in for PDFKit, which also walks every
object of the page for each tile it draws. Page objects are counted with form XObjects' contents.

| Sheet | File | Page (pt) | Page objects | Paths | Path segments | Chars | Render at fit (×0.15) | Render ×1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 06M composite (TP-6 passed) | 1.8 MB | 4235 × 3605 | 156,998 | 155,627 | 326,688 | 15,088 | 0.5 s | 0.7 s |
| **03 composite (lags)** | 15.5 MB | 5462 × 4777 | **1,999,744** | 1,992,604 | 4,019,420 | 61,457 | **7.4 s** | 7.3 s |
| Level 02 Concourse | 14.1 MB | 8682 × 5911 | 1,375,078 | 1,318,931 | 2,777,830 | 169,951 | 2.5 s | 2.6 s |

The 03 sheet draws 12.8 × the paths of 06M and pdfium takes ~10 × as long per frame; the cost does
not drop at low zoom (every path is still walked), which is why both the first draw and every
re-render while panning lag. Level 02 has 28,904 small images besides its paths.

## The raster (`server/app/jobs/plan_raster.py`)

| Sheet | dpi | Pixels | Tiles (512 px) | Raster PDF | Made in | Peak memory | Raster at fit (×0.25) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 03 | 200 | 15,173 × 13,269 (201 MP) | 780 | 24.4 MB | 12.6 s | 1.0 GB | 0.5 s (vector 7.4 s) |
| 02 | 158.9 (pixel cap) | 19,162 × 13,046 (250 MP) | 988 | 16.3 MB | 5.1 s | 0.9 GB | 0.55 s |
| 06M | — stays vector (157 K objects < 400 K) | | | | | | |

"Made in" is counting + rendering + JPEG on the PC; the Droplet's worker is slower. Most of the
peak is pdfium's parsed page (two million objects); one band of 2,048 rows is ~90 MB. The whole
03 upload in one process — text pass (95 s, 417 MB peak, 400 MB still held) then the raster
(18 s) — peaks at **1.28 GB**, under the worker's 2 GB cap (`docker-compose.prod.yml`, T124).

Chosen (D242): **400,000 page objects** (2.5 × the 06M sheet that passed TP-6), **200 dpi** (the
5.5 pt device labels ~15 px tall; 150 dpi is legible but soft), at most **250 MP** (≈ 1 GB decoded
if PDFKit holds every tile — Level 02 drops to 159 dpi), **512 px JPEG tiles at quality 75**
(4:2:0; 4:4:4 would add 31 % for crisper thin red hatching). The raster is larger than the
vector file (24 vs 15.5 MB for 03): a one-off download per level, prefetched on Wi-Fi.

Tiles, not one image: PDFKit draws the page tile by tile and skips an image outside the tile's
clip, so a pan at high zoom decodes a few 512-px tiles instead of a 200 MP image. Each tile carries
one pixel of its right and bottom neighbours, so no seam shows at fractional zooms
(`test_tiles_meet_without_seams`).
